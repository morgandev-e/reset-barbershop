The shop

A cut, a facial, a full reset.

Reset Barbershop is a grooming studio for men, women and children at Kettle House on Muthangari Road in Lavington. Alongside cuts, line-ups and colour, the menu covers hands, feet, facials and massage.

Book as a guest — no account needed — and pay at the shop. If you like, ask about a hot towel before your cut.
